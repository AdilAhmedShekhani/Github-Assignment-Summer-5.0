Our Solar System

A small trip around the sun and its planets

The Sun

The sun is a giant star at the centre of our solar system. It gives us light and heat, and without it there would be no life on earth.

The sun is so big that more than one million earths can fit inside it.

The Inner Planets

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest one to the sun. A year on Mercury is only 88 days long.

Venus

Venus is the hottest planet because its thick clouds trap the heat. It also spins in the opposite direction to most planets.

Earth

Earth is our home and the only planet we know that has life. About 70 percent of its surface is covered with water.

Mars

Mars is called the red planet because of the rusty dust on its surface. It has the tallest volcano in the solar system.

The Outer Planets

Jupiter

Jupiter is the biggest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a storm that has been going on for hundreds of years.

Saturn

Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings, which are made of ice and rock. It is so light that it could float on water.

Uranus

Uranus spins on its side, so it looks like it is rolling around the sun.

Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet and has the fastest winds in the solar system. It is a cold, deep blue colour.

Fun Facts

Light from the sun takes about 8 minutes to reach the earth.

The moon is slowly moving away from the earth every year.

There are more stars in space than grains of sand on all the beaches.

Look up at the sky tonight and enjoy the stars!
